Privacy

The short version

Beaver Apps (Beaver Reader and Beaver Focus) don't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your books, notes, stats and settings stay on your own device. If you turn on sync, they go to your own Google Drive or GitHub, straight from your device. The developer never receives them.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What stays on your device

The apps keep everything in your browser's storage on your device: your books and PDFs, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights and notes, reading and focus stats, and your settings. Nothing of this is sent anywhere unless you switch on one of the features below. Clearing the site's data in your browser (or removing the app) deletes it.

Sync with Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Sync with Google Drive in Beaver Reader, you sign in with Google and the app asks for one permission: drive.file. That lets it see and change only the files it creates itself, in a folder called Beaver Reader in your Drive. It cannot see any of your other files.

  • What goes there: your library (book details, progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, stats, shelves) and the book files you add, so your other devices can show the same bookcase.
  • How: the app talks to Google directly from your device. There is no Beaver Apps server in between, and nobody else, including the developer, can see your Drive or your data.
  • Google user data is used only to store and sync your own library for you. It is not sold, shared, used for ads or used to train any AI, and it is not transferred to anyone. Beaver Reader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • To stop: turn sync off in Settings, delete the Beaver Reader folder in your Drive if you like, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sync with GitHub (optional)

If you choose GitHub instead, you paste a GitHub token of your own into the app's Settings. It stays on your device and is used only to read and write your library in a repository you choose, directly between your device and GitHub.

Looking up a word (Beaver Reader)

When you select a word and tap Look up, only that word is sent to Wiktionary to fetch its definition. Nothing about you or your book is sent. Under More, Translate and Search open Google Translate or Google Search in your browser with the words you picked, and Share hands them to your phone's own share menu, only when you tap them.

Hosting

The apps are plain web pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Beaver Apps doesn't add any tracking of its own.

Children

The apps don't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact
